# School lookup review notes

## Review: Shepparton High, Sherbrooke and Siena

Reviewed 27 September 2026. These decisions concern the profile joined to each
annual VCE result, not a policy for aggregating predecessor and successor schools.
The remaining lookup entries have not been certified by this review.

### Shepparton High School

The [Victorian School Building Authority](https://www.schoolbuildings.vic.gov.au/greater-shepparton-secondary-college)
documents the formation of Greater Shepparton Secondary College in **2020** from
Shepparton High School, McGuire College, Wanganui Park Secondary College and
Mooroopna Secondary College. The new campus opened in 2022; this is not the merger
date.

The local ACARA profile workbook contains Shepparton High School, ID **45465**, in
Shepparton for every year from 2008 through 2019. Its VCE results in this dataset
are all pre-merger (2014–2019), so they require that historical school's profile.
The previous mapping to Sherbrooke Community School, ID 45316 in Sassafras, has no
support in the documented merger history.

Greater Shepparton Secondary College has ID **53105**, with populated profiles
from 2020 through 2024. The workbook also contains a 2019 placeholder for that ID
with no school type, ICSEA, enrolments or staff. A successor relationship does not
justify replacing the predecessor's annual profile with this placeholder or with
later school-wide data. Combining all four predecessors' result histories would
need a separate, explicit aggregation policy.

### Sherbrooke Community School

The [Public Record Office Victoria description, published through Research Data Australia](https://researchdata.edu.au/sherbrooke-community-school-no-6252/3752899)
states that the school was founded in 1985 and continued operating in 2025, with
2025–26 funding for planning modernised facilities. Its
[current school website](https://sherbrooke.vic.edu.au/) describes a government
Prep–12 school in Sassafras and carries 2026 school events.

The ACARA workbook independently contains ID **45316**, Government, Combined,
Sassafras, in every year from 2008 through 2024. These sources support continuity,
not a closure or merger into Siena College. The previous ID 45857 incorrectly
attached Siena's Catholic secondary-school profile and Camberwell coordinates.

### Siena College and Simonds Catholic College

[Dominican Education Australia's history of Siena](https://dominicaneducationaustralia.com/our-education-ministries/colleges-and-school/siena-college/)
records its opening on 2 February 1940 as Siena Convent, providing secondary
education for girls in Camberwell. Its
[current website](https://www.siena.vic.edu.au/) confirms the continuing school at
815 Riversdale Road, Camberwell.

[Melbourne Archdiocese Catholic Schools' history of Simonds](https://www.macs.vic.edu.au/SchoolInformation.aspx?School_ENumber=E1368)
records a different merger in 1996: Cathedral College East Melbourne and St Mary's
Boys' Regional School West Melbourne. It does not identify Siena as a predecessor.

The ACARA workbook has separate annual records for Siena College Ltd, ID **45857**,
Camberwell, and Simonds Catholic College, ID **45631**, West Melbourne, throughout
2008–2024. The sources support correcting Siena's mapping to 45857; Simonds retains
45631.

### Data verification

ID and year checks use `School Profile 2008-2024.xlsx`, sheet
`SchoolProfile 2008-2024`. Coordinates come from the existing annual ACARA location
workbooks. The 2025 VCE results have no same-year profile or location data and keep
those fields unavailable. The correction changes 30 analytical rows' IDs/context,
while preserving their source names, localities, years and all VCE result measures.
