How to read these rankings

Schools are ranked by the selected reported value, highest first. Equal values share a rank; tied schools are ordered alphabetically and included at the cutoff. These are rankings of one measure, not an overall rating of school quality.

Median study scores are out of 50. “Study scores 40+” is a percentage of study scores. ICSEA describes socio-educational context. Schools without the selected value are counted separately.

Data & methods

Understand what the figures measure and where they come from.

Results and school profiles have different coverage

The repository contains VCE results for 2014–2025. Its school profile and location fields extend through 2024. A missing value means unavailable in this dataset, and is never treated as zero.

Median VCE study score

The school’s reported median study score for that year. Study scores are out of 50. This is a school result, not an ATAR or an estimate of a school’s contribution to individual achievement.

Study scores of 40 and over

The percentage of study scores that are 40 or higher. The denominator is study scores, not students. Differences between percentages are expressed in percentage points.

School context

Sector, ICSEA and total enrolments come from ACARA school profiles. Total enrolments cover the whole school. ICSEA is a measure of socio-educational context, not an academic result. Every profile value should show its year.

Missing results and source notes

The existing loader merges source markers “–” and “I/D” into blank numeric values. Their separate meanings cannot be recovered from the analytical CSV alone. The future dataset should preserve those source states.
